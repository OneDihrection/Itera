# Garden interaction assets

Twemoji graphics by Twitter, Inc. and other contributors, licensed CC BY 4.0.
Source: https://github.com/twitter/twemoji
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Retrieved 4 November 2026 from the upstream master assets/svg directory:

- Butterfly: 1f98b.svg (unmodified).
- Leaf: 1f343.svg (runtime derivative removes wind marks).
- Seed: 1fad8.svg (runtime derivative isolates the first bean and changes the viewBox).

Upstream originals are preserved here with unmodified LICENSE-GRAPHICS. Runtime SVGs live in the corresponding garden-* asset catalogs. SwiftUI applies presentation transforms, including leaf folding and seed compression. Attribution and source/license links are available in the app's presentation settings. Assets are bundled; camera interaction needs no network.
