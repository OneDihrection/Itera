### Deskripsi

```text
Tuntun membantu pasien dan pendamping menjalani latihan gerak di rumah dengan langkah yang jelas dan tempo yang nyaman.

Pilih latihan tangan dan jari, lengan, menjangkau, berdiri, atau aktivitas sederhana seperti melipat handuk. Pendamping dapat mengatur sisi tubuh, jumlah pengulangan, dan waktu istirahat sesuai arahan tenaga kesehatan yang menangani pasien.

Saat latihan, Tuntun menampilkan contoh gerakan, memberi petunjuk suara dan teks, serta menggunakan kamera iPad untuk mengikuti gerakan dan menghitung pengulangan yang teramati. Latihan dapat dijeda, dilanjutkan, atau diakhiri. Setelah selesai, lihat ringkasan, riwayat latihan, dan perkembangan kegiatan dari waktu ke waktu.

Pilihan rekaman memungkinkan pendamping menyimpan cuplikan video singkat dari pengulangan penting, menyimpan tampilan kerangka gerak saja, atau tidak menyimpan tayangan ulang. Pengaturan awal menyamarkan wajah dan latar pada cuplikan video. Penyamaran otomatis dapat tidak sempurna; pilihan video tanpa samaran juga tersedia dengan persetujuan pasien.

Data latihan disimpan di perangkat. Tuntun tidak memerlukan akun dan tidak mengirim rekaman latihan ke layanan milik pengembang. Data yang disimpan di perangkat dapat termasuk dalam cadangan sistem iPad.

Tuntun adalah alat bantu latihan, bukan alat diagnosis atau pengukuran klinis. Perkiraan gerakan dari kamera dapat dipengaruhi posisi, cahaya, dan bagian tubuh yang tertutup. Gunakan program dan batas gerak yang dianjurkan tenaga kesehatan; hentikan latihan jika terasa nyeri atau tidak aman.

Dirancang untuk iPad dalam posisi mendatar. Tersedia dalam Bahasa Indonesia dan Inggris. Penggunaan kamera diperlukan untuk mengikuti gerakan; kontrol suara memerlukan izin mikrofon dan pengenalan ucapan pada perangkat.
```

### Description

```text
Tuntun helps patients and caregivers follow guided movement practice at home, one clear step at a time.

Choose hand and finger, arm, reaching, standing, or simple task practice such as folding a towel. Caregivers can set the side, repetitions, and rest time to match guidance from the patient's healthcare professional.

During a session, Tuntun shows movement examples and gives spoken and written cues. The iPad camera follows movement and counts observed repetitions. Pause, resume, or end a session when needed. Afterwards, review the session summary, practice history, and activity over time.

Recording choices let caregivers keep short video clips of selected repetitions, keep a movement skeleton only, or save no replay. By default, the app obscures faces and the background in saved clips. Automatic obscuring can miss identifying details; an unblurred option is also available with the patient's consent.

Practice data stays on the device. Tuntun does not require an account or send practice recordings to a developer-operated service. Data stored on the device may be included in iPad system backups.

Tuntun is a practice aid, not a diagnostic tool or a validated clinical measurement. Camera estimates can be affected by positioning, lighting, and occlusion. Follow the patient's clinician for a suitable plan and comfortable range; stop if movement is painful or unsafe.

Designed for iPad in landscape orientation. Available in Indonesian and English. Camera access is needed for movement tracking; voice controls require microphone and on-device speech recognition permission.
```

### Security Assessment — explanation

```text
Tuntun is an offline, single-patient iPad app for guided movement practice. It has no accounts, public feed, content upload or publishing, user-to-user messaging, or end-to-end encrypted instant messaging. It does not let users generate or distribute AI-generated or synthetic content. Its only prerecorded synthetic material is fixed exercise narration generated before release and included in the app bundle. Camera frames, voice-command audio, practice reports, and optional repetition clips are processed or stored locally and are not uploaded to a Tuntun service or made available to other users.
```

Apple also names **distribution of AI-generated or synthetic content** as an example that may require proof of a completed Security Assessment. Because it does not define whether distributing an app with fixed synthetic narration falls within that example, confirm applicability with Apple or qualified China mainland counsel before selecting **No assessment required**. If an assessment is required, obtain its proof and report from the National Internet Security Management Service Platform; explanatory text cannot substitute for those documents.

## App Privacy questionnaire — proposed answer

**Proposed selection: “No, we do not collect data from this app.”** Apple defines collection as transmission off the device in a way that lets the developer or a third party access data beyond a real-time request. The inspected app source contains no app network, analytics, advertising, account, or developer cloud integration. The app processes camera frames and optional voice commands on-device and stores plans, an optional patient name, summaries, activity history, and optional replays locally. Normal iPad system backups are separate from developer-operated collection. Recheck the final release binary and every integrated SDK before selecting this answer; a later upload, analytics SDK, support form, or service integration changes the response.
