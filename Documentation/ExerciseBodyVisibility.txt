EXERCISE BODY VISIBILITY
========================

Which body parts must be visible to the camera for each exercise.
"Required" parts must be detected in a frame or the frame is not used
(tracking is lost, holds freeze, nothing is credited). "Optional" parts
improve tracking but are estimated when missing.

Taken from the tracking code; source files are listed per exercise
(paths under Tuntun/Features/Therapy/).


GENERAL
-------
- iPad front camera for every exercise. Body exercises prefer the
  ultra-wide camera, hand exercises the wide-angle camera.
- Only one person in the picture. The patient is locked by torso position
  and shoulder width; a bystander is never adopted for being larger.
- Body frames are padded downward by 50% before inference, so the lower
  body near the bottom edge can still be found.
- Seated body exercises are framed from the waist up. Sit to stand is
  framed from the side, down to the knees.
- "Exercised side" means the arm selected for the activity (left or right).
  With both sides, the sides alternate, so both arms must fit in view.


BODY EXERCISES (Vision body pose)
=================================

1. ARM RAISE
   Source: Shared/Movement/MovementActivity.swift (limbJoints)
   Framing: seated, waist up.
   Required (exercised side):
   - Hip              (reference, confidence >= 0.3)
   - Shoulder         (pivot, confidence >= 0.3)
   - Elbow            (tracked point, confidence >= 0.2)
   Not needed: wrist, the other arm, face, legs.

2. ELBOW BEND
   Source: Shared/Movement/MovementActivity.swift (limbJoints)
   Framing: seated, waist up.
   Required (exercised side):
   - Shoulder         (reference, confidence >= 0.3)
   - Elbow            (pivot, confidence >= 0.2)
   - Wrist            (tracked point, confidence >= 0.2)
   Not needed: hip, face, legs.

3. DIRECTIONAL REACH
   Source: Activities/DirectionalReach/DirectionalReachActivity.swift
   Framing: seated, waist up, with room for the arm to reach right/left
   and up.
   Required:
   - Exercised shoulder, elbow and wrist
   - Both shoulders   (also used to detect mirroring / body orientation)
   - Nose (face)
   Not needed: hips, legs.

4. CROSS MIDLINE (Balancing)
   Source: Activities/Balance/CrossMidlineBalanceEngine.swift (geometry)
   Framing: seated, waist up.
   Required:
   - Neck or nose
   - Both shoulders
   - Wrist of the moving arm
   - Neck/nose AND mid-hip or one hip, freshly detected, to score a reach
   Optional: hips / mid-hip (estimated below the shoulders when missing,
   but scoring waits for fresh hip evidence).

5. EXTERNAL ROTATION, INTERNAL ROTATION, SHOULDER FLEXION (Balancing)
   Source: Activities/Balance/ArmMovementEngine.swift (geometry)
   Framing: seated, waist up.
   Required:
   - Neck or nose
   - Both shoulders
   - Elbow and wrist of the active arm (accepted at low confidence and
     slightly beyond the picture edge)
   Optional: hips / mid-hip (estimated below the shoulders when missing).
   If the body is found but the elbow or wrist is not, the app asks the
   patient to sit back (arm out of view).

6. SIT TO STAND (Balancing)
   Source: Activities/SitToStand/SitToStandActivity.swift
   Framing: patient sideways to the camera, full body down to the knees,
   both seated and standing.
   Required (side facing the camera, confidence >= 0.3):
   - Shoulder
   - Hip
   - Knee
   Not needed: ankle/feet, arms, face.


HAND EXERCISES (Vision hand pose)
=================================
Source: Activities/Fingers/FingerGeometry.swift (requiredJoints)
Joints need confidence >= 0.5 unless noted. "Knuckle" = MCP joint.

7. FULL FIST, HOOK FIST
   Required:
   - Wrist
   - At least two complete finger chains (knuckle, middle joint and end
     joint of the same finger); a fist hides some fingers, so two suffice.

8. THUMB OPPOSITION
   Required:
   - Wrist
   - Middle-finger knuckle
   - Thumb tip              (confidence >= 0.3)
   - Tip of the target finger (index, middle, ring or little;
     confidence >= 0.3)

9. FINGER SPREADING
   Required:
   - Wrist
   - Middle-finger knuckle
   - Index, middle, ring and little fingertips

10. FINGER EXTENSION SEQUENCE
    Required:
    - Wrist
    - The selected finger's knuckle, middle joint, end joint and tip

11. FOREARM ROTATION
    Required (confidence >= 0.3, because a turning palm hides knuckles):
    - Wrist
    - Index, middle and little-finger knuckles

12. FOLD TOWEL
    Source: Activities/FoldTowel/TowelSnapshot.swift
    Required (selected hand):
    - Thumb tip, index tip and middle-finger knuckle (pinch measurement)
    - At least three of: wrist, index, middle, ring and little-finger
      knuckles (palm-centre cursor that moves the towel)
    Also keep the towel's grab and goal areas inside the picture.
